### Crucial Operational Insight: How AUTh Anonymizes Submissions

Across the official jury chair reports—Denis Smalley (2017), Trevor Wishart (2019), Annette Vande Gorne (2021), Jonty Harrison (2023), and Kees Tazelaar (2025)—AUTh uses strict “pure acousmatic” blind judging across \~220–270 entries per edition [1](https://xenakis.web.auth.gr/results/), [3](https://xenakis.web.auth.gr/):

As Maronidis and Harrison explicitly state, names, countries, program notes, and even composition titles are withheld from the jury during judging (*“deliberations were purely acousmatic, based only on close and attentive listening to the works as they presented themselves in sound alone”*) [1](https://xenakis.web.auth.gr/results/), [3](https://xenakis.web.auth.gr/).

Why our package setup matters here:

Audio file hygiene is everything: Because the jury evaluates *only* the audio file, resampling your 48 kHz master to the required 44.1 kHz / 16-bit WAV with a \-1.84 dB inter-sample true-peak guard (0 clipped transients), stripping all embedded LIST/ID3 tags, and naming the file songname.wav (without your artist name) ensures zero DAW metadata leaks and zero sample-rate conversion artifacts on their playback rig.

How your PDF dossier is actually used: One single PDF combining Contact Info \+ Short Bio (Page 1\) and Program \+ Technical Notes (Page 2\) matches their exact intake workflow—the secretariat uses Page 1 for eligibility/archival verification now, and pulls your Short Bio \+ Program Note directly for the Results announcement and Noesis Planetarium Concert Booklet if your piece is selected among the 5–6 distinguished works [1](https://xenakis.web.auth.gr/results/), [3](https://xenakis.web.auth.gr/).

### . What Wording & Syntax Performs Best in This Context

Studying the published dossiers and concert booklets of past Xenakis winners (*Nikos Stavropoulos*, *Luis Quintana*, *Leah Reid*, *Cameron Naylor*, *Phivos-Angelos Kollias*) points to five clear conventions:

1. Analytical Perceptual Proposition First—Zero Liner-Note Hyperbole  
   * Avoid: Bandcamp/press verbs and adjectives (*“plunges the listener into…”*, *“razor-sharp”*, *“mesmerizing”*, *“demands immersion”*).  
   * Use: Direct perceptual and spatial framing from sentence one—defining the psychoacoustic threshold the piece investigates (*cross-modal coupling, electroacoustic space as a porous perceptual membrane rather than an external container*) in the same calm, authoritative register as your *The Ear Has No Interior*notes.
